Honeybees play a vital role in our global food supply.
These small insects are essential pollinators for one-third of the crops we eat every day, including fruits like apples, vegetables like broccoli, and nuts like almonds. As they travel from flower to flower gathering nectar,
pollen sticks to their bodies and rubs off onto other plants, allowing those crops to reproduce. Without this natural teamwork, grocery store shelves would lose a massive variety of fresh produce. Ultimately,
protecting bee populations is not just about saving an insect, but about safeguarding human agriculture and food security worldwide.
